# 📋 Scrum Quick Reference Cheatsheet

> A printable, at-a-glance reference for the entire Scrum framework.

---

## 👥 Roles

| Role | Core Responsibility | Key Accountability |
|------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Product Owner (PO)** | Maximizes product value; manages the Product Backlog | *Product Goal* |
| **Scrum Master (SM)** | Servant-leader; coaches the team; removes impediments | *Scrum adoption effectiveness* |
| **Developers** | Self-organizing; builds the Increment every Sprint | *Sprint Goal & Definition of Done* |

> [!NOTE]
> The Scrum Team is cross-functional and self-managing. No sub-teams, no hierarchies.

---

## 📅 Events (The 5 Events)

| Event | Who | Timebox (2-week Sprint) | Purpose |
|-------|-----|------------------------|---------|
| **The Sprint** | Scrum Team | 1–4 weeks | Container for all other events |
| **Sprint Planning** | Scrum Team | Max 4 hours | Define Sprint Goal + select + plan Backlog items |
| **Daily Scrum** | Developers | 15 minutes | Inspect progress toward Sprint Goal; adapt plan |
| **Sprint Review** | Scrum Team + Stakeholders | Max 2 hours | Inspect the Increment; update Product Backlog |
| **Sprint Retrospective** | Scrum Team | Max 1.5 hours | Inspect process; commit to improvements |

---

## 📦 Artifacts & Commitments

| Artifact | Contains | Commitment |
|----------|----------|------------|
| **Product Backlog** | Ordered list of everything needed in the product | **Product Goal** — the long-term objective |
| **Sprint Backlog** | Sprint Goal + selected items + the plan | **Sprint Goal** — why this Sprint has value |
| **Increment** | All completed Backlog items that meet DoD | **Definition of Done** — quality standard |

---

## 🗓️ Sprint at a Glance (2-Week Sprint)

```
Day 1     → Sprint Planning (4h max)
           → Team selects items, creates Sprint Backlog

Days 2–9  → Development + Daily Scrum (15 min/day)
           → Developers self-organize to meet Sprint Goal

Day 10    → Sprint Review (2h max)
           → Demo working Increment to stakeholders

Day 10    → Sprint Retrospective (1.5h max)
           → Improve team process for next Sprint

Day 11    → New Sprint begins immediately
```

## 📝 User Story Format

```
As a [type of user],
I want [some goal],
So that [some reason/benefit].
```

**Example:**
> As a *registered user*, I want to *reset my password via email*, so that *I can regain access to my account without contacting support*.

---

## ✅ INVEST Criteria (Good User Stories)

| Letter | Meaning | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **I** | Independent | Can be developed and delivered independently |
| **N** | Negotiable | Details can be discussed and changed |
| **V** | Valuable | Delivers value to the user or business |
| **E** | Estimable | Team can estimate the effort |
| **S** | Small | Fits within one Sprint |
| **T** | Testable | Has clear acceptance criteria |

---

## 🔢 Story Points Scale (Fibonacci)

| Points | Complexity | Rough Effort |
|--------|-----------|--------------|
| **1** | Trivial | < 1 hour |
| **2** | Simple | Half a day |
| **3** | Small | 1 day |
| **5** | Medium | 2–3 days |
| **8** | Large | 1 week |
| **13** | Very Large | Consider splitting! |
| **21+** | Epic | Must be split into smaller stories |

---

## 📖 Key Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Backlog Refinement** | Ongoing process of adding detail, estimates, and order to Product Backlog items |
| **Definition of Done (DoD)** | Formal description of the state of the Increment when it meets required quality measures |
| **Epic** | A large user story too big for a single Sprint; must be broken down |
| **Sprint Goal** | A single objective for the Sprint that gives the team focus and flexibility |
| **Velocity** | Amount of work (story points) completed in a Sprint; used for forecasting |
| **Impediment** | Anything blocking the team from progress; the SM is responsible for removing it |
| **Increment** | The sum of all completed Product Backlog items that meet the DoD |
| **Product Goal** | The long-term objective for the Scrum Team; exists in the Product Backlog |

## ⚠️ Common Scrum Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | What It Looks Like | The Fix |
|-------------|-------------------|---------|
| **Scrum-but** | "We do Scrum, but we skip Retrospectives" | Follow the framework fully, then adapt |
| **Zombie Standup** | Daily Scrum is a status report to the manager | Refocus on Sprint Goal progress |
| **No Real Retro** | Retrospective ends with zero action items | Commit to at least 1 concrete improvement |
| **PO overrides team** | PO dictates how much work team must take | Team self-selects Sprint Backlog items |
| **Hardening Sprint** | A Sprint dedicated only to fixing bugs/tech debt | Quality must be built in every Sprint |
| **Big User Stories** | Stories that span multiple Sprints | Split into smaller, INVEST-compliant stories |
